How to read this

  • Source: Google Sheet Production Dashboard → Production_Data, fetched live — no manual update needed.
  • Idle hrs (not running) — hours in the shift where the line has no production entry. These are the “machine stood idle” hours.
  • Below-target hrs — hours where the line did produce but stayed under target (Achievement % < 100). This is under-performance, not idle time.
  • On-target hrs — hours produced at or above target.
  • Reason — the Remarks written against the below-target hours.
  • An hour appears in only one column. A day with no entries (e.g. a holiday) shows no rows for that date.
